## What's Included in v0.7.0

### 1. Enterprise Ride Tracking & Telemetry Engine (Task 5)
- **Ride Lifecycle State Machine**: Full `START`, `PAUSE`, `RESUME`, `STOP`, and `AUTO_STOP` state transitions.
- **Waypoint & Telemetry Recording**: Low-latency batch GPS telemetry ingestion storing `latitude`, `longitude`, `altitude`, `speed`, `bearing`, `heading`, `accuracy`, `provider`, `batteryLevel`, and `networkStatus`.
- **Spatial 2dsphere Indexing**: High-performance GeoJSON `Point` spatial indexing on `startLocation` and `endLocation` supporting `$nearSphere` radius queries.
- **Calculations Engine**: Automatic calculation of Total Distance, Moving Distance, Total Duration, Moving Time, Stopped Time, Average Speed, Moving Avg Speed, Max/Min Speed, Average Pace (min/km), Elevation Gain/Loss, Max/Min Elevation, and Ride Safety Score (0–100).
- **Open Standard Track Exports**:
  - **GPX Export**: `<gpx>` XML format with `<trkpt>` trackpoints (`/api/v1/rides/:id/export/gpx`).
  - **GeoJSON Export**: Standard `FeatureCollection` / `LineString` format (`/api/v1/rides/:id/export/geojson`).
  - **CSV Export**: Tabular telemetry data format (`/api/v1/rides/:id/export/csv`).
- **Animated Ride Replay Engine**: Keyframes animation payload generator for smooth client-side trajectory playback.
- **Rider Aggregate Analytics**: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly distance/ride counters, longest ride, fastest ride, and highest elevation.
- **Real-Time Socket.IO Streaming**: Broadcasting `ride:started`, `ride:paused`, `ride:resumed`, `ride:finished`, `location:updated`, and `telemetry:updated` events.
