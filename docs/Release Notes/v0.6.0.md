## What's Included in v0.6.0

### 1. Digital Garage Subsystem (Task 4)
- **Motorcycle CRUD**: Support for unlimited bikes per rider (`/api/v1/bikes`).
- **Default Vehicle Switching**: Auto-assignment and explicit default vehicle toggling.
- **Maintenance Logging**: Tracking service categories (`OIL_CHANGE`, `CHAIN_SERVICE`, `BRAKE_SERVICE`, etc.) with cost, odometer reading, and next service due targets.
- **Fuel Log & Economy Engine**: Automatic calculation of distance since last fill (`KM`) and fuel economy (`Kmpl`) with rolling average update.
- **Expense Consolidation**: Auto-generation of service and fuel expenses into a consolidated financial log.
- **Vehicle Health Score**: Dynamic health score algorithm (0–100) factoring expired insurance, PUC, registration, and active maintenance status.
- **Garage Fleet Statistics**: Aggregated total distance traveled, total maintenance/fuel expenditure, and average fleet health score.
